CLINICAL WORKFLOW

A clear sequence from shade to polish.

01

SELECT

Choose the shade before dehydration; assess value, chroma and opacity.

02

ISOLATE

Create a clean, moisture-controlled operating field.

03

BOND

Condition and apply the approved adhesive protocol for the case.

04

PLACE

Build anatomy in controlled increments according to the approved IFU.

05

FINISH

Refine contours, verify occlusion and complete the polishing sequence.

LAYERING

Build value, chroma and opacity with intention.

DENTIN

Body shade establishes chroma and internal form.

ENAMEL

A controlled outer layer refines value and translucency.

OPAQUE

OA2 supports masking and localized opacity control.

For a simple single-shade restoration, begin with the closest body shade. For complex aesthetic work, combine body, enamel and opaque strategies according to the clinical situation.
